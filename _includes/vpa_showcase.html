Solution #1 · Flagship

Vision-Predictor-Action (VPA)

The bottleneck: why current AI robots hesitate

  • Most robot foundation models (VLAs) reuse a large language model and emit movement coordinates one token at a time, like writing a sentence word by word.
  • Every extra token is another full pass through a billion-parameter decoder, so latency grows with the length of the motion.
  • In practice that means control loops of only a few hertz (often under 5 Hz): the arm pauses mid-motion, overshoots, and drops objects.

The VPA breakthrough

  • No token loop. VPA removes the autoregressive decoder entirely and controls the robot in a compact latent space.
  • Milestones + math. A neuro-symbolic planner sets the next visual milestone; a flow-matching solver computes the full continuous motion toward it.
  • Constant cost. Each decision is a fixed K + 3 network evaluations, no matter how long the motion chunk is.
