Why robot arms hesitate, and what VPA changes

The bottleneck in today's robot models

  • Most robot foundation models (vision-language-action models, VLAs) reuse a large language model and emit movement coordinates one token at a time, like writing a sentence word by word.
  • Every extra token is another full pass through a billion-parameter decoder, so latency grows with the length of the motion.
  • That means control loops of only a few hertz: the arm pauses mid-motion, overshoots and drops objects.

What VPA does instead

  • No token loop. VPA removes the autoregressive decoder and controls the robot in a compact latent space.
  • Milestones plus a motion solver. A neuro-symbolic planner sets the next visual milestone; a flow-matching solver computes the whole continuous motion toward it.
  • Constant cost. Each decision is a fixed K + 3 network evaluations, however long the motion chunk is.
