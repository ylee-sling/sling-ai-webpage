Business model

Embodied AI-as-a-Service: two revenue paths, one flywheel

Edge

Edge Runtime Software

The high-frequency VPA runtime engine, licensed to robot OEMs and factory-automation builders. It runs on the robot's own edge chip, needs no cloud round-trip in the control loop, and fits in under 75 MB.

  • Per-robot or per-deployment runtime license
  • Integration support for custom arms, grippers, and camera rigs
  • Task-specific fine-tuning on the customer's demonstrations
Cloud

Cloud Fleet Management & Continuous Learning

A subscription service that turns every field failure into a better model for the whole fleet:

  1. Capture edge-case failures on deployed robots
  2. Upload the episode to the cloud
  3. Simulate it across thousands of synthetic variations
  4. Retrain the VPA policy on the expanded data
  5. Redeploy over-the-air (OTA) to every robot
