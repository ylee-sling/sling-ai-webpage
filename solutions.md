# Solutions: Deterministic Logic for High-Stakes Automation
{: .fs-9 }

## Combining Generative AI with Formal Mathematical Rigor
{: .fs-6 .fw-300 }

Modern high-stakes automation faces a persistent challenge: **unpredictability**. Standard artificial intelligence models rely primarily on statistical probabilities, which can introduce stochastic errors, spatial hallucinations, and operational risks. In critical domain operations—such as navigating 5-axis CNC machine paths, synthesizing FPGA hardware architectures, or processing legal documentation—probabilistic accuracy alone is often insufficient.

**SLING AI** addresses complex industrial challenges through the framework of mathematical logic. By integrating generative AI with formal verification methods—such as **Separation Logic** and **SMT Solvers**—we translate physical, hardware, and textual workflows into discrete, mathematically verifiable systems. 

> **Our Focus:** Prioritizing safety, verifiable determinism, and reliable outputs for cyber-physical, hardware, and legal automation workflows.
